# RawLight Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

RawLight is a photo library and raw editor that runs entirely on your device.

**What we collect:** nothing. RawLight has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your photos, edits and settings stay on your device (and in your iCloud backup if you have device backups on).

**Photos:** RawLight reads only the photos you choose to import, and saves only the photos you export. Photo library access is used for nothing else.

**On-device AI:** subject, object and denoise processing run on your device. Your photos are never uploaded.

**Optional model download:** if you choose to download the AI denoise model, RawLight fetches one file from github.com over HTTPS. That request does not include your photos or any personal information, and GitHub may log your IP address under its own policy.

**Children:** RawLight does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
