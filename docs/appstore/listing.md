# App Store listing draft

**Name:** RawLight  (check availability in App Store Connect and run a trademark search first)
**Subtitle (30):** Raw photo editor for iPad
**Category:** Photo & Video
**Age rating:** 4+
**Price:** set in App Store Connect (Apple takes 15% under the Small Business Program, otherwise 30%)

**Promotional text:** Edit raw photos on iPad and iPhone with on-device AI masks, no account and no uploads.

**Description:**
RawLight is a fast, non-destructive raw photo editor and library for iPad and iPhone.

- Open raw files from Sony, Canon, Nikon, Fujifilm and more, plus JPEG, PNG and HEIC
- Light, colour, curves, detail, effects, optics and crop with straighten and level
- Masks: Subject, Sky, Background, Object tap, Lasso, Linear, Radial, Brush and more
- Select with a tap or draw a loop with Apple Pencil, powered by an on-device model
- Optional AI denoise for raw files (downloaded only if you ask)
- Ratings, flags, albums, filters and swipe gestures
- Copy and paste edits, history, before and after
- Your photos never leave your device. No account, no tracking

**Keywords (100):** raw,photo editor,lightroom alternative,develop,mask,denoise,exposure,curves,crop,dng,pencil

**Support URL / Privacy URL:** use the GitHub pages for this repo until you have a site
**Review notes:** No login required. Sample photos are included in the app's demo library. The AI denoise model is an optional download from github.com.
